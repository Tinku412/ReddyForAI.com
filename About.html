Hey there! I'm Sharath ✌️

As a product manager, I love connecting dots between what businesses need and how tech can solve them. When I'm not in meetings or working, you'll find me playing with AI tools and dreaming up new ways to make life easier.


I always wanted a side project, something to do over the weekend. So, with all the buzz around AI tools, I thought why not create a simple project from scratch completely with the help of AI, and see how far it can go.


This is a simple website to discover, share and learn about AI image prompts. It is a collection of prompts that I have found interesting and wanted to recreate. It is a work in progress and I am open to suggestions and contributions.
